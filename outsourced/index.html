Outsourced

Every question you ask an AI scores a point for AI. Every search you run yourself scores one for your brain.

A Chrome extension that keeps the score for today, this week, the last 4 weeks or all time, and makes a card you can share.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store.

What scores

A point for AI

Each question you send to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, DeepSeek, Grok, Le Chat or Google's AI Mode.

A point for Brain

Each search on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo or Brave Search. Paging through results or switching to Images is still one search.

A score on day one

Outsourced can read your last 90 days of browser history once, if you allow it, and hands the access back the moment the count is done.

What it keeps

  • Only the counts and when each point was scored. Never what you type or search.
  • No account, no servers, no analytics, no ads.
  • The extension isn't allowed to connect to the internet at all. Its security policy blocks every connection.
  • Your score stays in your browser. Removing Outsourced deletes it.
