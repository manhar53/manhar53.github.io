Privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Outsourced is a browser extension that counts the questions you send to AI chatbots and the searches you run yourself, and shows the score. This policy explains what it keeps and where that goes. In short: only counts are kept, in your browser, and nothing is ever sent anywhere.

What it keeps

  • How many points AI and Brain scored, per day, per site, and per quarter of the day.
  • When the last point was scored.
  • Your settings, such as whether the toolbar icon shows today's score.
  • For about 30 minutes, a one-way hash of each recent search, so paging through one search counts once. It's held in session storage and cleared when the browser closes. The search itself is never stored.
  • If you run the optional 90-day check: the counts it produced. No history entries are kept.

All of this is stored only in your browser. Removing the extension deletes it, and Settings has a button that resets it.

What it never does

  • It never stores or sends what you type to an AI or what you search for. On AI sites, the text of a message you send is compared with the page in memory, only to confirm that the message was sent, and then discarded.
  • It never sends anything anywhere. There are no servers, accounts, analytics or ads, and the extension's security policy blocks every network connection.
  • It never sells or shares data. There is no data to share.

Permissions

  • Storage: to keep the score and settings on your computer.
  • Access to AI chat sites and search results pages: to add a point when you send a message or run a search. No other website is accessed.
  • History (optional): asked for only when you click "Check my last 90 days", used once to count AI chats and searches, then handed back straight away.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
